# 8. AWS Glue Workflows Evaluation Criteria

Compare **Glue Workflows** vs **MWAA**, **Step Functions**, and **Glue jobs without workflow**.

**Rating scale:** 1 (weak) — 5 (excellent)

## Scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | Glue Workflows | MWAA | Step Functions | Standalone Glue jobs |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Glue-native integration** | High | 5 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **Catalog / lakehouse fit** | High | 5 | 3 | 3 | 3 |
| **Orchestration idle cost** | High | 5 | 2 | 5 | 5 |
| **Complex multi-service DAGs** | High | 1 | 5 | 3 | 1 |
| **Visual authoring (low-code)** | Medium | 5 | 2 | 4 | 3 |
| **Portable orchestration code** | Medium | 1 | 5 | 1 | 2 |
| **Event-driven fit** | Medium | 3 | 3 | 5 | 2 |
| **Lineage in Glue Studio** | Medium | 5 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| **Backfill / operational UX** | Medium | 2 | 5 | 2 | 2 |
| **Enterprise IAM / LF** | High | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 |

## Decision matrix

| If priority is… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| Medallion entirely in Glue + catalog | **Glue Workflows** |
| Glue + Redshift + EMR + SaaS | **MWAA** |
| S3 event → Glue + Lambda notify | **Step Functions** (+ optional WF) |
| 2–3 independent scheduled jobs | Standalone triggers (no workflow) |
| Hybrid enterprise platform | **Glue Workflows** (in-Glue) + **MWAA** (platform) |
