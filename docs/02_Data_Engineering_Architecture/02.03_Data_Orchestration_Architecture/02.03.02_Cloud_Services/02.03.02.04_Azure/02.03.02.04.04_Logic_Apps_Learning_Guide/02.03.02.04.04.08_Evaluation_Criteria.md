# 8. Azure Logic Apps Evaluation Criteria

Compare **Logic Apps** vs **ADF**, **Step Functions**, and **Cloud Workflows**.

**Rating scale:** 1 (weak) — 5 (excellent)

## Scorecard

| Criterion | Weight | Logic Apps | ADF | Step Functions | Cloud Workflows |
| --- | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: | ---: |
| **Serverless / idle cost (Consumption)** | High | 5 | 5 | 5 | 5 |
| **SaaS / Microsoft connectors** | High | 5 | 3 | 2 | 2 |
| **Large-scale ETL** | High | 1 | 5 | 2 | 2 |
| **Event-driven ops glue** | High | 5 | 3 | 5 | 5 |
| **VNet / private integration** | Medium | 4 | 4 | 4 | 4 |
| **Developer experience (data eng)** | Medium | 3 | 4 | 3 | 3 |
| **Human approval workflows** | Medium | 5 | 2 | 4 | 4 |
| **Portable definition** | Medium | 2 | 2 | 1 | 1 |
| **Enterprise IAM** | High | 4 | 4 | 5 | 4 |
| **Lineage / data ops** | Medium | 2 | 5 | 2 | 2 |

## Decision matrix

| If priority is… | Choose |
| --- | --- |
| ADF failure → Teams | **Logic Apps Consumption** |
| Nightly 30-activity ELT | **ADF** |
| AWS-native serverless | **Step Functions** |
| GCP event glue | **Cloud Workflows** |
| Azure hybrid platform | **Logic Apps + ADF** |
