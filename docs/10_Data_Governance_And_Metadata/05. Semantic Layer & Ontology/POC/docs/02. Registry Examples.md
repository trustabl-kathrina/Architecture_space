# Registry Examples (TM Forum)

Canonical pilot concepts in `global`, seeded from TM Forum SID Tier A.

Data: `tmforum.json` → `concepts`.

| concept_id | SID domain | SID ABE | Example URI |
| --- | --- | --- | --- |
| party | Common | Party | `…/global/party` |
| party-role | Common | PartyRole | `…/global/party-role` |
| customer | Customer | Customer | `…/global/customer` |
| customer-account | Customer | CustomerAccount | `…/global/customer-account` |
| product | Product | Product | `…/global/product` |
| product-offering | Product | ProductOffering | `…/global/product-offering` |
| product-specification | Product | ProductSpecification | `…/global/product-specification` |
| customer-facing-service | Service | CustomerFacingService | `…/global/customer-facing-service` |
| product-order | Customer | ProductOrder | `…/global/product-order` |
| agreement | Common | Agreement | `…/global/agreement` |

### Example record — Customer

```yaml
concept_id: customer
uri: https://semantics.enterprise/ns/global/customer
namespace: global
kind: entity
preferred_label: Customer
bootstrap_source: tmforum-sid
annotations:
  sid_domain: Customer
  sid_abe: Customer
  sid_version: R20.0
status: approved   # after Governance
```
