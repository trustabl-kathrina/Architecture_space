## Objections (short answers)

- **“We already have Collibra.”** — Collibra stays SoR for glossary prose; the Grid is SoR for *enterprise meaning* and crosswalks.
- **“Will NATCOs lose ownership?”** — No. Catalogs stay local; they federate to global SID concepts.
- **“Is OSSIE our new repository?”** — No. Packages only — interchange, not a second SoR.
- **“Too big.”** — Pilot one domain (Customer) + one NATCO against global.

## Close

“Approve a Customer-domain pilot: Registry + Namespace, five maps, one product bind, Ossie export — then expand.”
