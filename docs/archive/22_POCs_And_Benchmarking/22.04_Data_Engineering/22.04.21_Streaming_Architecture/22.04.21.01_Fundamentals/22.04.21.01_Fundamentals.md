# Streaming Architecture Fundamentals

This section covers the core theoretical and conceptual foundations of Streaming and Event-Driven Architectures (EDA). 
Understanding these principles is critical before evaluating specific technologies (like Kafka, Flink, or Pub/Sub) or designing complex data meshes.

## Sub-Domains
1. **01_Event-Driven Architecture**: Core concepts of decoupling, Event Notification vs Event-Carried State Transfer, Choreography vs Orchestration.
2. **02_Streaming vs. Batch**: Architectural differences between bounded and unbounded data processing, Continuous vs Micro-batching.
3. **03_Event Time vs. Processing Time**: Handling out-of-order data, skew, time domains, and NTP considerations.
4. **04_Watermarks**: Tracking event time progress, the Idle Partition problem, and watermark propagation.
5. **05_Windowing**: Grouping unbounded streams into finite chunks (Tumbling, Sliding, Session), Triggers, and Evictors.
6. **06_Exactly-Once Semantics (EOS)**: Understanding message delivery guarantees, Chandy-Lamport distributed snapshots, and End-to-End EOS constraints.
7. **07_Streaming Design Patterns**: Established architectural patterns like CQRS, Event Sourcing, Outbox, DLQ, and Stream Joins.
