# ADR-002: Enterprise Data Strategy Approach

## Status

Accepted

## Date

2026-06-18

## Context

The enterprise requires a unified data strategy balancing central standards with domain autonomy.

## Decision

Adopt a federated data mesh strategy with enterprise-wide governance standards and domain-owned data products.

## Consequences

### Positive
- Aligns business and technology ownership
- Enables self-service at scale

## Alternatives considered

| Alternative | Why not chosen |
| Centralized data lake only | Bottleneck for domain agility |
