# Apache Kafka Architecture

Apache Kafka is a distributed event streaming platform capable of handling trillions of events a day. It is the de facto standard for enterprise streaming architectures. This section dives deep into the internal mechanics and architectural best practices for deploying, designing, and operating Kafka at scale.

## Sub-Domains
1. **Kafka Core Concepts**: Brokers, topics, consumer groups, and the log structure.
2. **Topic Design**: Strategies for modeling domains, naming conventions, and retention.
3. **Partition Strategies**: Ensuring parallel processing, order guarantees, and avoiding data skew.
4. **Replication and HA**: Designing for fault tolerance, ISRs, and leader election.
5. **Schema Registry**: Managing data contracts and enforcing schema evolution compatibility.
6. **Kafka Connect**: Integrating Kafka with external databases and systems without writing code.
7. **Kafka Streams**: Stateful stream processing native to the Kafka ecosystem.
8. **Security and Governance**: Securing clusters via TLS, SASL, ACLs, and Quotas.
