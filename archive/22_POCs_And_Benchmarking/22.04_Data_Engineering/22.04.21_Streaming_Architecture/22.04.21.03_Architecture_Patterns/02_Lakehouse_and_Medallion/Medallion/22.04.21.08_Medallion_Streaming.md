# Medallion Streaming Architecture

The Medallion Architecture (Bronze, Silver, Gold) is typically associated with batch Lakehouses. Applying this logically to pure streaming pipelines provides a structured way to handle data quality and enrichment in real-time.

## Sub-Domains
1. **Bronze Streaming**: Raw ingestion and buffering.
2. **Silver Streaming**: Deduplication, validation, and enrichment.
3. **Gold Streaming**: Real-time aggregations and business logic.
4. **Incremental Processing**: Strategies for efficiently updating state.
5. **Real-Time Data Products**: The final output layer for consumers.
