# Security and Governance

Securing event streams goes beyond TLS and passwords. It encompasses data privacy, compliance, and tracking the lineage of data as it flows through the enterprise.

## Sub-Domains
1. **Event Governance**: Establishing ownership and lifecycle management for topics.
2. **Data Lineage**: Tracking origin and transformations.
3. **Schema Governance**: Enforcing data contracts.
4. **Data Quality**: Validating streams in flight.
5. **PII Handling**: Ensuring privacy compliance (GDPR/CCPA).
6. **Compliance**: Audit logging and access controls.
