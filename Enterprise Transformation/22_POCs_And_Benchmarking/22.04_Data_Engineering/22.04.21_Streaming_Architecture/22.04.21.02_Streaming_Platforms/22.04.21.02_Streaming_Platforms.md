# Streaming Platforms

This domain breaks down the core technologies used in streaming architectures. To maintain a clean taxonomy, technologies are strictly divided into two distinct responsibilities:

1.  **Event Streaming (The Storage/Broker Layer)**: The platforms responsible for ingesting, buffering, and routing raw data.
2.  **Stream Processing (The Compute Layer)**: The engines and frameworks responsible for transforming, aggregating, and joining the unbounded data.

Both sections are further categorized by native cloud vendor offerings vs. open-source ecosystems.

## Directory Structure
*   **01_Event_Streaming**: Kafka, Kinesis, Pub/Sub, Event Hubs.
*   **02_Stream_Processing**: Flink, Spark Streaming, Beam, Dataflow, Stream Analytics, Kafka Streams.
