# Proof of Concepts (POCs) & Benchmarking

This section documents the strategies, methodologies, and outcomes of architectural POCs designed to evaluate streaming technologies against specific enterprise requirements. It follows the same structural taxonomy as the Streaming Platforms domain.

## Directory Structure

1. **01_Event_Streaming_Bakeoffs**: Comparing raw message brokers (e.g., Kafka vs. Pub/Sub).
2. **02_Stream_Processing_Bakeoffs**: Comparing compute engines (e.g., Flink vs. Spark vs. Dataflow).
3. **03_Performance_Benchmarks**: Framework-agnostic tests for Latency and Throughput under load.
4. **04_CDC_Benchmarks**: Evaluating database impact and replication lag for CDC tools.
5. **05_Capability_Comparisons**: Feature matrices comparing Cloud-Native vs. Open-Market streaming technologies.
