# Streaming Architecture Diagrams

Visual representations of the streaming architectures, ranging from high-level logical flows to detailed sequence diagrams for specific edge cases.

## Diagram Categories
1. **Logical Architecture**: High-level component interactions (e.g., Sources -> Broker -> Processor -> Sink). Independent of specific technologies.
2. **Physical Architecture**: Deployment topologies, VPCs, subnets, Kubernetes clusters, and Multi-AZ/Multi-Region broker placements.
3. **Sequence Diagrams**: Step-by-step flows for complex interactions, such as the Transactional Outbox pattern or exactly-once transactional commits between Flink and Kafka.
4. **Event Flow Diagrams**: Mapping the lifecycle of a specific business entity (e.g., an Order) as it mutates across multiple topics and microservices.
5. **Reference Blueprints**: Standardized, approved templates for common patterns (e.g., "Standard CDC to Snowflake Pipeline") to be used by domain teams.

*(Note: Actual diagram files (e.g., draw.io, Mermaid.js, or image files) should be placed in the respective subfolders).*
