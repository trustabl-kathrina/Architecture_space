# What is Enterprise Architecture?

## Definition
Enterprise Architecture (EA) is the strategic discipline that translates business vision and strategy into effective enterprise change by creating, communicating, and improving the key requirements, principles, and models that describe the enterprise's future state and enable its evolution.

## The Modern EA Shift
Traditional EA focused heavily on rigid frameworks (TOGAF) and slow governance. Modern EA acts as an **Enterprise Decision System**:
- **From** Gatekeepers **To** Enablers.
- **From** Document-heavy **To** Code-heavy and Platform-heavy.
- **From** IT-aligned **To** Business-aligned.

## Core Domains of Modern EA
1. **Business Architecture**: Capabilities, Value Streams.
2. **Data & Information Architecture**: Data Mesh, Data Products, Lakehouse, Semantics.
3. **Application & Integration Architecture**: Microservices, SaaS, Event-Driven.
4. **Technology Architecture**: Cloud-Native, Kubernetes.
5. **AI & Agentic Architecture**: LLMs, Agents, AI Platforms.
6. **Security Architecture**: Zero Trust, Privacy Engineering.
