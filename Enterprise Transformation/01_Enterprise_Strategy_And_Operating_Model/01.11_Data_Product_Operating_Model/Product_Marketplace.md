# Product Marketplace

## Concept
An internal "App Store" for Enterprise Data.

## Capabilities
- **Search & Filter**: Find products by domain, tag, or usage tier.
- **Checkout Experience**: A streamlined workflow to request access.
- **Automated Provisioning**: If access is approved by the Product Owner, the platform automatically grants IAM permissions (no manual IT tickets).
- **Reviews & Ratings**: Consumers can rate data products, driving accountability and visibility into product quality.
