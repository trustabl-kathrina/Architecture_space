# Product Owner Model

## The Data Product Owner (DPO)
Every Data Product must have a named owner. This is typically a business-aligned role, not an IT engineer.

## Responsibilities
- Understands consumer needs and defines the product roadmap.
- Accountable for data quality and meeting published SLAs.
- Manages the budget and resources for the data product team.
- Approves or denies access requests to the data product.

## Cultural Shift
Moving accountability from the centralized data engineering team to the business domain generating the data.
