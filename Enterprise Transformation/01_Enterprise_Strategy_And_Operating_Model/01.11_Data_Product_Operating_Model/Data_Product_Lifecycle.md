# Data Product Lifecycle

## Overview
Treating data as a product means it has a lifecycle, exactly like a software application or a physical good.

## Stages
1. **Discovery & Ideation**: Identifying a business need that requires data.
2. **Design**: Defining the Data Contract, schema, and SLAs.
3. **Development**: Engineering the data pipeline using the self-serve platform.
4. **Publishing**: Registering the product in the internal Marketplace/Catalog.
5. **Operations (Run)**: Monitoring quality, fulfilling SLA obligations, and supporting consumers.
6. **Iteration**: Upgrading the product (v1.0 -> v2.0) based on consumer feedback.
7. **Retirement**: Deprecating the product when it is no longer used or relevant.
