# Product Funding Model

## Strategy
Shift from project-based IT funding to continuous product-based funding.

## Mechanism
- **Baseline Funding**: Teams receive a baseline budget to run the product and meet SLAs.
- **Value-Driven Growth**: Products that show high adoption across the enterprise (many downstream consumers) receive increased funding for enhancements.
- **Deprecation**: Products with zero consumers lose funding and are decommissioned.
