# Product Discovery Process

## Problem Space
Consumers often don't know what data exists, leading to duplicated engineering efforts.

## Solution
- **Enterprise Data Catalog / Marketplace**: The central search engine for all enterprise data products.
- **Rich Metadata**: Products must be tagged with business terms, domains, classifications, and contact information for the Product Owner.
- **Sample Data**: Providing safe, anonymized samples so consumers can evaluate the product before requesting full access.
