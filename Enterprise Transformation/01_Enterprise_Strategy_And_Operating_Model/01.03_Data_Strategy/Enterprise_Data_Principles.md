# Enterprise Data Principles

1. **Data is Owned**: Every critical data asset must have a clear business owner (Data Steward/Product Owner).
2. **Data is Shared**: Data should be shared across the enterprise by default, restricted only by legal/security requirements.
3. **Data is Fit for Purpose**: Data quality must match the requirements of its intended use case.
4. **Data is Standardized**: Use common vocabularies and taxonomies (Business Glossary) across the enterprise.
5. **Data is Secured**: Protect data at rest, in transit, and in use, following Zero Trust principles.
