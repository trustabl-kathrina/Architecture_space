# Enterprise Data Sharing Strategy

## Internal Sharing
- Moving away from point-to-point ETL integrations.
- Implementing an Internal Data Marketplace for discovering and requesting access to Data Products.
- Utilizing Data Contracts to manage expectations between producers and consumers.

## External Sharing
- Utilizing modern data sharing protocols (e.g., Delta Sharing, Snowflake Secure Data Sharing) to share data securely with partners and vendors without copying files.
- Establishing strict governance and privacy controls for external egress.
