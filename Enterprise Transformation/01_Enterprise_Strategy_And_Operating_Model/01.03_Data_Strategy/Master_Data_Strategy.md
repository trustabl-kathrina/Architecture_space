# Master Data Strategy

## Overview
Creating a single, trusted, 360-degree view of critical business entities (e.g., Customer, Product, Supplier).

## Approach
- Identify critical data elements (CDEs) across the enterprise.
- Implement an MDM platform to resolve identities, match, merge, and manage golden records.
- Integrate MDM with operational systems to enforce data quality at the point of entry.
- Publish Master Data as core Enterprise Data Products for analytical use.
