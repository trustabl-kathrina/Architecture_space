# Data-Driven Organization

## Characteristics
- Decisions are based on empirical data, not just intuition.
- Data is available at the point of decision (embedded analytics).
- Transparency in how metrics are calculated.

## Cultural Shifts
- From "IT owns the data" to "Business owns the data."
- From "Hoarding data" to "Sharing data."
- From "Reactive reporting" to "Predictive insights."
