# AI Mission

## Mission Statement
To build a scalable, secure, and governed Enterprise AI Ecosystem that empowers business domains to safely leverage predictive, generative, and agentic AI capabilities to solve complex business problems.

## Key Objectives
- Establish an Enterprise AI Platform with guardrails for safe experimentation and production deployment.
- Identify and execute high-ROI AI use cases aligned with corporate strategy.
- Develop the internal talent and operating model required to manage an AI-native workforce.
