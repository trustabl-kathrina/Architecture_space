# AI Operating Model

## Overview
How the enterprise organizes teams and processes to deliver AI.

## The Hub and Spoke Model
- **Central AI Center of Excellence (The Hub)**: Owns the AI platform, sets governance standards, evaluates foundational models, and provides deep ML expertise.
- **Business Domains (The Spokes)**: Identify use cases, own the domain data context, and deploy AI solutions using the paved roads provided by the CoE.

## Key Roles
- AI Product Manager
- Prompt/Context Engineer
- ML Engineer / LLMOps Engineer
- AI Ethicist / Governance Lead
