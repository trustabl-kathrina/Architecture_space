# Agentic AI Strategy

## Overview
The strategy for deploying autonomous AI agents capable of reasoning, planning, and executing actions using enterprise tools.

## The Agentic Evolution
1. **Copilots**: AI assists humans (Human drives, AI suggests).
2. **Supervised Agents**: AI executes tasks, human approves (AI drives, Human reviews).
3. **Autonomous Agents**: AI executes tasks independently within bounded guardrails.
4. **Multi-Agent Systems**: Ecosystems of specialized agents collaborating to solve complex problems.

## Strategic Focus
- Standardizing the **Tool/Action Registry** (giving agents secure APIs to interact with business systems).
- Developing robust **Agent Memory Systems** (Short-term context, Long-term enterprise memory).
- Implementing **Human-in-the-Loop (HITL)** governance protocols.
