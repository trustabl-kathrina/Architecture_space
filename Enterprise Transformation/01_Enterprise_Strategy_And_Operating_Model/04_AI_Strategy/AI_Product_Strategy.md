# AI Product Strategy

## Paradigm Shift
Moving from treating AI as a "project" or "model" to treating AI as a "Product".

## AI Product Management
- Defining the user problem, not just the technical metric (e.g., Accuracy vs. User Satisfaction).
- Managing the continuous lifecycle (models degrade over time, products must be maintained).
- Tracking Product SLAs, performance metrics, and business ROI continuously.
