# AI Adoption Model

## Overview
Strategy for driving user adoption and managing the cultural change associated with AI.

## Enablement Strategy
- **Digital Literacy Programs**: Educating the workforce on how to interact effectively with AI (e.g., Prompt Engineering 101).
- **Change Champions**: Identifying early adopters within business units to advocate for AI tools.
- **Trust Building**: Transparently communicating the capabilities and limitations of deployed AI systems.
