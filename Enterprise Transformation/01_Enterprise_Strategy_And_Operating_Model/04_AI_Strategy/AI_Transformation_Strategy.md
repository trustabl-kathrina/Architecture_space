# AI Transformation Strategy

## Overview
The approach to evolving the organization's capabilities, culture, and technology to fully leverage Artificial Intelligence.

## The Transformation Journey
1. **Ad-Hoc / Exploration**: Shadow AI, unstructured experimentation with LLMs.
2. **Foundational**: Establishing the AI CoE, securing data, deploying internal secure chat interfaces.
3. **Embedded (AI-Enabled)**: Integrating predictive models and RAG applications into existing business workflows.
4. **Agentic (AI-Native)**: Deploying autonomous agents capable of planning, tool usage, and multi-step execution.
5. **Autonomous Enterprise**: Orchestrating ecosystems of human and AI digital workers.

## Key Strategic Enablers
- AI-Ready Data Foundation (Knowledge Graphs, Vector Stores).
- Scalable Platform Engineering (LLMOps/MLOps).
- Robust AI Risk and Governance frameworks.
