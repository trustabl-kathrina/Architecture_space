# Future State Architecture

## Overview
The high-level blueprint of what the enterprise will look like post-transformation.

## Core Paradigms
- **Cloud-Native**: Shift from on-premise servers to elastic, managed cloud services.
- **Data Mesh/Fabric**: Shift from centralized monolithic data lakes to federated, domain-owned data products.
- **AI-Native**: Shift from traditional software applications to agentic workflows and intelligent systems.
- **Platform Engineering**: Shift from ticket-based IT provisioning to self-service developer platforms.

## Purpose
Acts as the "North Star" to guide all technology purchasing and build vs. buy decisions.
