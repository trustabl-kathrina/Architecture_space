# Execution Roadmap

## Overview
The master artifact that sequences all initiatives, platforms builds, migrations, and organizational changes over a multi-year horizon.

## Key Elements
- **Timelines & Milestones**: Clear quarter-by-quarter deliverables.
- **Dependencies**: Critical path mapping (e.g., "Cannot build AI Churn Model until Customer MDM is established").
- **Resource Allocation**: Mapping budget and teams to the timeline.

## Living Document
The roadmap is not static. It must be reviewed and adjusted quarterly based on business shifts, technology evolution, and PI Planning outcomes.
