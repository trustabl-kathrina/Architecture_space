# Change Management

## Overview
Technology transformation is easy; people transformation is hard. Change Management addresses the human element of adopting new architectures.

## Core Activities
- **Stakeholder Analysis**: Understanding who is impacted and their level of resistance.
- **Communication Plan**: Transparent, continuous updates on "why" the change is happening and "what's in it for me" (WIIFM).
- **Training & Enablement**: Upskilling staff on new cloud, data, and AI tools.
- **Incentive Alignment**: Updating KPIs and bonus structures to reward adoption of the new operating model.
