# Data Product Funding

## Overview
Funding the lifecycle of a Data Product in a Data Mesh architecture.

## Approach
- **Seed Funding**: Initial budget to build the product and establish the data contract.
- **Run Funding**: Continuous OpEx budget to maintain the product, handle data quality, and support consumers.
- **Value-Based Adjustments**: Funding is increased or decreased based on the product's adoption (number of consumers) and the business value generated by those consumers. If a data product has no consumers, its funding is cut and it is deprecated.
