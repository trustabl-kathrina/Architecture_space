# FinOps Model

## Overview
Financial Operations (FinOps) is the practice of bringing financial accountability to the variable spend model of cloud and AI.

## The Trinity of FinOps
1. **Inform**: Visibility into allocation, benchmarking, and forecasting.
2. **Optimize**: Right-sizing, using reserved instances, optimizing queries.
3. **Operate**: Continuous alignment of engineering, business, and finance teams to execute optimization.

## Relevance
Essential for preventing "cloud shock" and controlling the spiraling costs of Generative AI inference.
