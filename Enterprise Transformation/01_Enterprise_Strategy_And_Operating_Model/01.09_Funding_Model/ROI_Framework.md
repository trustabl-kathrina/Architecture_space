# ROI Framework

## Overview
Methodology for calculating the Return on Investment for Data and AI initiatives.

## Components
1. **Cost (The Investment)**: Infrastructure, talent, licenses, maintenance.
2. **Hard Value**: Direct revenue generated or costs eliminated (e.g., reduced headcount via automation).
3. **Soft Value**: Risk avoidance (e.g., preventing a compliance fine), brand equity, or increased employee satisfaction.

## Measurement
Establishing baseline metrics before project initiation and continuously tracking them post-deployment.
