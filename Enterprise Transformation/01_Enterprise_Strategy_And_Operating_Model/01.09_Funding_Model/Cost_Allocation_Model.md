# Cost Allocation Model

## Definition
The methodology used to distribute shared IT, Data, and Cloud costs across the business units that consume them.

## Key Challenges
In cloud and shared platform environments, attributing costs accurately to specific business initiatives is complex without proper tagging and architecture.

## Strategy
Implement robust resource tagging and FinOps practices to ensure every compute cycle, storage byte, and API call can be traced back to a specific business domain, product, or cost center.
