# Enterprise Maturity Model

## Purpose
To provide a quantifiable method for assessing the organization's current capabilities and tracking progress toward the target state.

## Maturity Levels

### Level 1: Siloed & Reactive (Ad Hoc)
- Fragmented data and shadow IT.
- Manual, error-prone processes.
- No enterprise-wide strategy for Data or AI.

### Level 2: Managed & Standardized (Developing)
- Centralized data warehouses/lakes.
- Standardized IT processes (ITIL/Traditional DevOps).
- Emerging centers of excellence for Data/AI.

### Level 3: Proactive & Integrated (Defined)
- Cloud-first architecture.
- Adoption of DataOps and MLOps.
- Enterprise-wide data governance and API strategy.

### Level 4: Predictive & Product-Oriented (Advanced)
- Data Mesh and Data Products established.
- Platform Engineering providing self-service capabilities.
- Pervasive use of predictive and generative AI.

### Level 5: Autonomous & AI-Native (Optimized)
- Agentic AI systems managing complex workflows.
- Active Metadata and continuous observability.
- Fully automated governance and FinOps.

## Assessment Dimensions
Maturity is assessed across the following dimensions:
1. **Strategy & Leadership**
2. **People & Culture**
3. **Operating Model**
4. **Data & Analytics**
5. **AI & Automation**
6. **Technology & Infrastructure**
7. **Security & Governance**
