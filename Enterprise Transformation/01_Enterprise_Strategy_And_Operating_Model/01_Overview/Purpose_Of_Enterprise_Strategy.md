# Purpose of Enterprise Strategy

## Executive Summary
Enterprise Strategy serves as the north star for the organization's transformation journey. It bridges the gap between high-level business objectives and execution, ensuring that all technology, data, and AI investments are directly aligned with business value creation.

## Core Objectives
1. **Alignment**: Ensure business, data, and technology strategies are moving in unison.
2. **Value Creation**: Shift focus from cost-center IT to value-generating digital capabilities.
3. **Prioritization**: Provide a framework for investment decisions and resource allocation.
4. **Transformation**: Guide the organization from its current operating model to a future-ready, AI-native enterprise.

## The Strategy to Execution Linkage
```text
Business Strategy → Business Capabilities → Operating Model → Data & AI Strategy → Architecture → Execution
```

## Key Deliverables
- Enterprise Vision and Mission
- Strategic Business Objectives (OKRs)
- Target Operating Model
- Transformation Roadmaps
- Value Realization Frameworks

## Success Metrics
- Percentage of IT/Data initiatives directly tied to strategic business OKRs.
- Time-to-market for new digital products.
- Adoption rate of strategic platforms.
- Measurable ROI from Data and AI investments.
