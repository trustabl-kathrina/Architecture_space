# Adoption Strategy

## Overview
A newly built platform (e.g., a Data Mesh or an Internal Developer Platform) is useless if no one uses it.

## Tactics
- **The "Paved Road"**: Make the new platform significantly easier and faster to use than the legacy process.
- **Evangelism**: Use early adopters (from the Quick Wins) to champion the platform to other business units.
- **Forced Deprecation**: Set hard dates for turning off legacy systems to force migration, but only after the new platform is proven stable.
