# Quick Wins

## Importance
Transformations require momentum. Quick wins build executive trust, secure further funding, and prove that the new target architecture works.

## Criteria for a Quick Win
- **High Visibility**: Solves a known pain point for a key business sponsor.
- **Low Complexity**: Can be delivered in 6-12 weeks without requiring the entire future-state platform to be built first.
- **Measurable ROI**: Clear metrics showing cost reduction or revenue increase.

## Examples
- Replacing a highly manual daily reporting process with an automated cloud dashboard.
- Deploying a Generative AI Copilot for customer service agents using existing knowledge base documents.
