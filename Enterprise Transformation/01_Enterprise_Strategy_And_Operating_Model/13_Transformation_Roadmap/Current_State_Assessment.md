# Current State Assessment

## Overview
Before defining a roadmap, the enterprise must objectively baseline its current capabilities, technical debt, and operating model.

## Key Assessment Areas
1. **Business Alignment**: How well does current IT/Data support business goals?
2. **Architecture**: Review of legacy monoliths, data silos, and point-to-point integrations.
3. **Data Quality & Governance**: Assessment of trust in data, manual workarounds, and compliance risks.
4. **Talent & Culture**: Assessment of cloud/data/AI skills, agile maturity, and resistance to change.
5. **Cost (FinOps)**: Understanding the baseline TCO of existing infrastructure.

## Deliverables
- Heatmaps of current capabilities.
- Technology debt inventory.
- Process bottleneck analysis.
