# Digital Business Model

## Overview
The transition from traditional, analog business models to digital-first models.

## Characteristics of the Digital Model
- **Ecosystems & Platforms**: Moving from linear supply chains to orchestrating digital ecosystems.
- **As-a-Service**: Transitioning from one-off product sales to recurring subscription services (requires robust billing and usage data architectures).
- **Data Monetization**: Treating data as a direct revenue-generating asset rather than an exhaust byproduct.
- **Omnichannel**: Blurring the lines between physical and digital interactions.

## Architectural Implications
The Digital Business Model requires an API-first, event-driven, and highly scalable cloud architecture to succeed.
