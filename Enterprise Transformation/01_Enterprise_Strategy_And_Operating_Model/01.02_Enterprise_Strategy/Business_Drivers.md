# Business Drivers

## Overview
Business drivers are the internal and external forces that compel the organization to transform and adopt new Data & AI architectures.

## External Drivers
- **Market Disruption**: New digitally native competitors entering the market.
- **Customer Expectations**: Demand for real-time, highly personalized digital experiences.
- **Regulatory Pressure**: Increasing complexity of data privacy, security, and AI regulations.
- **Technological Advancements**: The rapid maturation of Generative AI and Agentic systems.

## Internal Drivers
- **Cost Pressures**: The need to reduce operational overhead through automation.
- **Data Silos**: Inability to generate cross-functional insights due to fragmented data.
- **Legacy Technical Debt**: Outdated systems hindering agility and speed-to-market.
- **Talent Scarcity**: The need to maximize the productivity of existing knowledge workers.
