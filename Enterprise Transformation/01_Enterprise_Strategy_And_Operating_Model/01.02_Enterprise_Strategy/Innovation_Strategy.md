# Innovation Strategy

## Purpose
Define how the organization explores, evaluates, and adopts emerging technologies to maintain a competitive edge.

## Innovation Pillars
1. **Horizon 1 (Core Optimization)**: Improving current processes using mature tech (e.g., RPA, basic BI).
2. **Horizon 2 (Adjacent Expansion)**: Adopting maturing tech to build new capabilities (e.g., GenAI Copilots, Lakehouse).
3. **Horizon 3 (Transformational)**: Exploring frontier tech (e.g., Agentic Workflows, Quantum Computing, Autonomous Enterprises).

## Innovation Operating Model
- **Innovation Labs**: Dedicated teams testing Horizon 2 & 3 technologies.
- **Rapid Prototyping (POCs)**: Strict timeboxes (4-6 weeks) to prove value.
- **Scale-Out Process**: Framework to transition successful POCs into enterprise-grade production platforms.
