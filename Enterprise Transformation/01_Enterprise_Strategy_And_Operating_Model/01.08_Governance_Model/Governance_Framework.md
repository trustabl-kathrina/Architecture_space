# Enterprise Governance Framework

## Overview
The Enterprise Governance Framework provides the overarching structure for how decisions are made, policies are enforced, and risks are managed across Data, AI, Technology, and Business processes.

## Core Objectives
1. **Enablement**: Governance should accelerate value delivery, not block it, by providing clear, safe paths for execution.
2. **Trust**: Ensure all digital assets are secure, compliant, and reliable.
3. **Alignment**: Ensure execution adheres to the Enterprise Target State Architecture.

## Key Pillars
- **Data Governance**: Managing data quality, privacy, and lifecycle.
- **AI Governance**: Ensuring ethical, unbiased, and secure AI systems.
- **Architecture Governance**: Maintaining technical standards and interoperability.
- **Security & Risk Governance**: Protecting the enterprise from cyber and operational threats.
