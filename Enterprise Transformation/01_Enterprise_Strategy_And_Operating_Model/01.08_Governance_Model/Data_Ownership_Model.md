# Data Ownership Model

## Principle
Data is owned by the business function that creates it, not by the IT department that stores it.

## Responsibilities of a Data Owner
- Defining access rights and security classifications.
- Ensuring data quality at the point of capture.
- Approving Data Contracts for downstream consumption.
- Resolving data-related issues and escalations.

## Challenge
Establishing true accountability requires cultural change and formal performance metrics tied to data quality.
