# AI Governance Model

## Overview
Ensuring AI systems are safe, effective, and aligned with corporate ethics and regulatory requirements.

## Lifecycle Governance
- **Intake & Triage**: Assessing the risk and value of proposed AI use cases.
- **Development**: Guardrails for data usage and model training.
- **Validation**: Red-teaming, bias testing, and performance benchmarking before deployment.
- **Production Monitoring**: Continuous tracking for model drift, data drift, and hallucinations.

## Key Boards
- AI Ethics Committee.
- AI Architecture Review Board.
