# AI Service Model

## Overview
How AI capabilities are provisioned and supported across the enterprise.

## Tiers of Service
1. **Self-Service (PaaS/SaaS)**: Business users consume AI via secure Chat interfaces or by building their own low-code Copilots using enterprise platforms.
2. **Enabled Service**: CoE provides specialized architecture guidance and pre-built RAG templates for a domain team's engineers to implement.
3. **Full Service**: The CoE builds and deploys a highly complex custom model end-to-end on behalf of a business unit.
