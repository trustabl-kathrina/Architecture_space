# AI Product Model

## Overview
Just like Data Products, AI capabilities (Models, APIs, Agents, Copilots) must be treated as lifecycle-managed products, not one-off projects.

## Core Concepts
- **AI Product Manager**: Bridges the gap between business needs and data science/engineering capabilities.
- **Continuous Delivery**: AI models degrade over time (data drift, concept drift). They require continuous monitoring, retraining, and redeployment.
- **Consumer-Centric**: AI products must provide clear, measurable value to the end user (e.g., reducing handle time in a call center).
