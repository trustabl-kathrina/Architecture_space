# AI Factory Model

## Definition
An industrialized approach to discovering, developing, and deploying AI solutions at enterprise scale.

## The Assembly Line
1. **Intake & Triage**: Standardized process for business units to submit ideas.
2. **Experimentation (The Lab)**: Timeboxed (e.g., 4 weeks) feasibility studies to prove value and data availability.
3. **Engineering (The Build)**: Transforming notebooks/POCs into robust, scalable software engineering artifacts.
4. **Operations (The Run)**: Deploying to production using MLOps/LLMOps pipelines and monitoring performance.

## Objective
Reduce the time it takes to move an AI concept into a production reality from months to weeks.
