# Product Governance

## Overview
Ensuring Data Products comply with enterprise standards without slowing down domain autonomy.

## Federated Computational Governance
- **Global Standards**: Defined by central governance (e.g., naming conventions, PII tagging requirements).
- **Automated Enforcement**: The self-serve platform blocks a Data Product from being published to the Marketplace if it fails automated compliance checks (Policy-as-Code).
- **Local Autonomy**: Domain teams are free to model the data and design the logic as long as they pass the automated gates.
