# Data Product Definition

## What is a Data Product?
A Data Product is a highly curated, trustworthy, and addressable data asset managed by a cross-functional team to serve a specific business need. It is the fundamental architectural node of a Data Mesh.

## Core Characteristics (The DATSIS principles)
- **Discoverable**: Easily found in the Enterprise Data Catalog.
- **Addressable**: Accessible via standard APIs or query endpoints.
- **Trustworthy**: Backed by SLAs, data quality metrics, and clear ownership.
- **Self-Describing**: Contains comprehensive metadata, semantics, and lineage.
- **Interoperable**: Adheres to global enterprise standards for joining with other products.
- **Secure**: Governed by centralized, automated access policies.
