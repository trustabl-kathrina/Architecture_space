# Product Metrics

## Overview
How to measure the success and health of a Data Product.

## KPIs
- **Adoption**: Number of unique downstream consuming applications or teams.
- **Engagement**: Frequency of queries or API calls.
- **Reliability**: SLA adherence percentage.
- **Time to Access**: The duration from a consumer requesting access to receiving usable data.
- **Consumer Satisfaction (CSAT)**: Survey scores from internal users.
