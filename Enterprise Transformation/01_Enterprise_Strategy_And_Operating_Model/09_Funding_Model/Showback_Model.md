# Showback Model

## Overview
A softer approach to cost allocation where IT provides "receipts" to business units showing what they consumed, but no actual budget transfer occurs.

## Purpose
- Creates cost visibility and awareness.
- Drives conversations around optimization without penalizing experimentation.

## Transition
Many enterprises use Showback as a stepping stone. Once tagging, measuring, and reporting mature over 12-18 months, the organization may transition to a formal Chargeback model.
