# Platform Funding Model

## Overview
How the central Platform Engineering team (providing self-service infrastructure, data platforms, etc.) is funded.

## The Problem
Platform teams don't generate direct business value; they enable domains to generate value. If they rely on project funding, the platform degrades.

## Solution
Treat the platform as a corporate utility or internal product:
- Funded centrally as an enterprise OpEx baseline.
- OR funded by taking a "tax" or percentage from the domain teams' product budgets, acknowledging that the platform saves them money on infrastructure engineering.
