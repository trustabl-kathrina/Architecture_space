# Chargeback Model

## Overview
An aggressive cost allocation model where business units are internally billed (in "hard dollars" transferred between budgets) for the IT, Cloud, and Data resources they consume.

## Pros
- Drives highly responsible consumption behavior.
- Clearly demonstrates the cost of shadow IT or inefficient queries.

## Cons
- Administratively heavy.
- Can discourage experimentation and innovation if domains are afraid of incurring costs for POCs.

## Best Practice
Use sparingly, typically for highly mature organizations with precise FinOps capabilities.
