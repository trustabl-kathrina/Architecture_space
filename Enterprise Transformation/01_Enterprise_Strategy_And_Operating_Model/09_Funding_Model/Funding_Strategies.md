# Funding Strategies

## Overview
How the enterprise finances its Data, AI, and Technology transformations. Traditional IT project funding models often fail modern agile, product-centric organizations.

## Traditional vs. Modern Funding
- **Traditional (Project-Based)**: CapEx heavy, funded for a specific duration, teams disband after launch. Leads to technical debt and orphaned systems.
- **Modern (Product-Based)**: OpEx heavy, continuous funding for a persistent team managing a product lifecycle.

## Strategic Shift
Transitioning from funding "systems" to funding "business capabilities" and "data products."
