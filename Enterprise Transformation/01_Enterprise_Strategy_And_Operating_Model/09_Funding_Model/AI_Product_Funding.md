# AI Product Funding

## Overview
Funding models for AI initiatives, particularly LLMs and Agentic systems, which have unique cost structures.

## Cost Considerations
- **Development**: High initial CapEx for talent and specialized compute.
- **Inference (Run)**: Continuous OpEx based on token usage or GPU uptime.

## Strategy
- Centralized funding for the core AI Platform / Foundation Models.
- Business unit funding for the inference costs of the specific applications (e.g., Copilots) they deploy, driving accountability for prompt optimization and usage.
