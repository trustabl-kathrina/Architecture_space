# Data Product Strategy

## Definition
Treating data as a product involves applying product management principles to data sets, ensuring they are discoverable, addressable, trustworthy, self-describing, interoperable, and secure.

## Key Components
- **Data Product Owner**: Accountable for the lifecycle and quality of the data product.
- **Data Contracts**: Explicit agreements on schema, semantics, and SLAs.
- **Marketplace**: A central hub where consumers can find and subscribe to Data Products.

## Transition
Shifting funding and team structures from "building pipelines" to "managing data products."
