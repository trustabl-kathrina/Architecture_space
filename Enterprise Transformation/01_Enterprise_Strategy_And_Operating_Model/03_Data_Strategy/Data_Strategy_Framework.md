# Data Strategy Framework

## Overview
The Data Strategy Framework defines how data will be managed, governed, architecture, and utilized to achieve business objectives. It shifts data from an IT byproduct to a strategic enterprise asset.

## Core Pillars
1. **Data Governance & Quality**: Ensuring data is trusted, secure, and compliant.
2. **Data Architecture**: The physical and logical design of data systems (Lakehouse, Mesh).
3. **Data Management & Operations**: How data is ingested, stored, and moved (DataOps).
4. **Data Analytics & AI**: How data is consumed to generate value.
5. **Data Culture & Literacy**: Enabling the workforce to understand and utilize data.

## Guiding Principles
- Data is a shared enterprise asset.
- Data must be easily discoverable and accessible.
- Security and Privacy are built-in, not bolted on.
- Single version of truth for master data.
