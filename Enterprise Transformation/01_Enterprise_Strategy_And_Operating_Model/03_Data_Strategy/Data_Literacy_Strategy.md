# Data Literacy Strategy

## Definition
The ability of the workforce to read, work with, analyze, and communicate with data.

## Enablement Plan
- **Assess**: Evaluate current literacy levels across different business units.
- **Train**: Develop targeted training programs (e.g., basic dashboard usage for operational staff, advanced SQL/Python for analysts).
- **Community**: Establish Data Communities of Practice (CoPs) to share knowledge and best practices.
- **Tools**: Deploy user-friendly, self-service tools that match the users' literacy levels.
