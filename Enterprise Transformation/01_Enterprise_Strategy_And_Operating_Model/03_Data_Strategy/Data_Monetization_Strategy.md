# Data Monetization Strategy

## Overview
How the enterprise generates financial value from its data assets.

## Types of Monetization
1. **Internal Monetization (Indirect)**:
   - Cost reduction through process optimization.
   - Revenue increase through personalized marketing and pricing optimization.
2. **External Monetization (Direct)**:
   - Selling data sets or insights to third parties.
   - Creating new digital products/services powered by proprietary data.
   - Enhancing existing products with data features (e.g., benchmarking reports for B2B clients).
