# Governance Strategy

## Overview
Data Governance ensures that data is secure, private, accurate, available, and usable.

## Operating Model
Transitioning to a **Federated Governance Model**:
- Centralized definition of global policies, standards, and compliance rules.
- Decentralized execution and stewardship by domain data owners.

## Key Focus Areas
- Data Quality Management
- Data Privacy & Security
- Master & Reference Data Management
- Semantic Standardization (Business Glossary)
