# Data Mesh Strategy

## Overview
A decentralized sociotechnical approach to managing enterprise data at scale.

## The Four Pillars
1. **Domain-Oriented Decentralized Data Ownership**: Business domains (e.g., Sales, HR) own and manage their data.
2. **Data as a Product**: Domains serve their data as high-quality products to the rest of the enterprise.
3. **Self-Serve Data Infrastructure as a Platform**: A centralized platform team provides the tools for domains to build and serve data products easily.
4. **Federated Computational Governance**: Ensuring global standards and interoperability while allowing local autonomy.
