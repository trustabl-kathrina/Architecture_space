# Data as an Asset

## Concept
Data must be treated with the same rigor and management as financial or physical assets. It has intrinsic value that depreciates if not maintained and appreciates when combined with other data.

## Asset Management Practices
- **Inventory**: Maintaining a comprehensive Enterprise Data Catalog.
- **Valuation**: Measuring the ROI of data products and analytics use cases.
- **Maintenance**: Investing in ongoing data quality and master data management.
- **Protection**: Implementing robust security, access controls, and backup strategies.
