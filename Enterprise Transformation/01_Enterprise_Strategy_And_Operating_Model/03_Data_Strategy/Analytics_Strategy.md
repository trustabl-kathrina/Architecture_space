# Analytics Strategy

## Evolution
Moving the enterprise from descriptive reporting to predictive and prescriptive analytics.

## Key Initiatives
- **Self-Service BI**: Empower business users to create their own insights using governed, semantic layers.
- **Advanced Analytics**: Centralized capabilities for ML and AI model development.
- **Embedded Analytics**: Integrating dashboards and insights directly into operational applications (e.g., CRM).
- **Decision Intelligence**: Using AI to recommend actions based on analytical findings.
