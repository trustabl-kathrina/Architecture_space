# Governance Maturity Model

## Levels of Maturity

### Level 1: Ad Hoc
Governance is reactive, undocumented, and relies on heroic individual efforts.

### Level 2: Siloed
Governance exists but is departmental. IT-driven data governance with heavy manual processes.

### Level 3: Standardized
Enterprise-wide policies exist. Business stewards are identified. Tools like Data Catalogs are deployed but require manual upkeep.

### Level 4: Federated
Business domains take full ownership. Central CoE sets policies. Data is treated as a product.

### Level 5: Computational & Autonomous
Governance is fully integrated into the platform via Policy-as-Code. Active metadata and AI agents automatically classify data, monitor drift, and enforce access controls.
