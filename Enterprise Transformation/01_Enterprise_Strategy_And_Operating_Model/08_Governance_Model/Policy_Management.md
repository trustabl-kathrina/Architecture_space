# Policy Management

## Overview
The lifecycle of creating, communicating, enforcing, and updating enterprise policies.

## Policy Hierarchy
1. **Principles**: High-level enduring truths (e.g., "Data is a shared asset").
2. **Policies**: Mandatory rules (e.g., "All external data sharing requires approval").
3. **Standards**: Specific technical requirements (e.g., "Use AES-256 for encryption").
4. **Guidelines**: Recommended best practices (e.g., "Naming conventions for tables").

## Automation
Transitioning from written PDF documents to machine-readable rules enforced automatically in CI/CD pipelines and data platforms.
