# Compliance Framework

## Overview
Ensuring all technological and data activities adhere to external laws and internal regulations.

## Key Regulatory Domains
- **Data Privacy**: GDPR, CCPA, HIPAA.
- **Financial**: SOX, PCI-DSS.
- **AI Specific**: EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF.

## Architectural Enablers
- Comprehensive Data Lineage (proving where data came from and went).
- Immutable Audit Logs.
- Automated Consent Management systems.
