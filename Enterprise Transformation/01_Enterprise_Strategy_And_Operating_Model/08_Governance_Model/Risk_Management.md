# Risk Management

## Overview
Identifying, assessing, and mitigating risks associated with digital, data, and AI initiatives.

## Risk Categories
- **Strategic Risk**: Investing in the wrong technology or failing to adopt AI.
- **Operational Risk**: System downtime, broken data pipelines, inaccurate AI models.
- **Compliance Risk**: Violations of GDPR, CCPA, EU AI Act.
- **Reputational Risk**: Customer backlash from biased AI decisions or data breaches.

## Mitigation
Maintained via an Enterprise Risk Register, continuous monitoring, and the implementation of Zero Trust architecture.
