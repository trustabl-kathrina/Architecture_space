# Data Governance Model

## Overview
How the enterprise defines, manages, and protects its data assets.

## The Shift
Moving from restrictive, IT-led data governance to federated, business-led, and automated computational governance.

## Key Components
- **Business Glossary & Taxonomy**: Establishing a common enterprise language.
- **Data Quality Management**: Defining and monitoring rules for critical data elements.
- **Master Data Management (MDM)**: Governing the golden records.
- **Lifecycle Management**: Archival and retention policies.

## Implementation
Execution relies on Active Metadata systems to automate classification, lineage tracking, and policy enforcement at the platform level.
