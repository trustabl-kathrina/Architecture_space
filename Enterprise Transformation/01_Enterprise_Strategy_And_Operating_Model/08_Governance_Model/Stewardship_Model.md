# Stewardship Model

## Overview
Data Stewards are the operational executors of Data Governance. They bridge the gap between IT and the Business.

## Types of Stewards
1. **Business Data Steward**: Subject matter experts defining business rules, glossary terms, and quality requirements.
2. **Technical Data Steward**: IT personnel executing the technical implementation of rules and pipelines.
3. **Data Owner**: The senior business leader ultimately accountable for the data domain's quality and compliance.

## Modern Evolution
In a Data Mesh, the traditional "Steward" role is often absorbed into the responsibilities of the **Data Product Owner**.
