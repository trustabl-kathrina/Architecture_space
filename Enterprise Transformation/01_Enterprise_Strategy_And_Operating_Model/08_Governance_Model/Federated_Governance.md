# Federated Computational Governance

## Definition
A model where global policies are defined centrally but execution and ownership are distributed to local business domains.

## The "Computational" Aspect
To make federation work at scale without massive overhead, governance must be encoded into the platform (Policy-as-Code). 

## Examples
- A central policy states: "All PII must be encrypted at rest."
- The platform automatically scans schemas, tags PII using AI, and applies encryption policies via code before the data product is published.
- The domain owner is accountable, but the platform automates the compliance.
