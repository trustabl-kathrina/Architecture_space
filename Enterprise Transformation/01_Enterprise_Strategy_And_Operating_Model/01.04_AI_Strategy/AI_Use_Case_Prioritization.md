# AI Use Case Prioritization

## Prioritization Framework
Use cases are evaluated using a standardized matrix balancing **Business Value** against **Feasibility/Risk**.

## Assessment Criteria

### 1. Business Value
- Revenue Generation (New products, cross-sell).
- Cost Reduction (Process automation, efficiency).
- Customer Experience (NPS improvement, resolution time).
- Strategic Alignment.

### 2. Feasibility & Risk
- **Data Readiness**: Is the required data available, high-quality, and contextualized?
- **Technical Complexity**: Can current platforms support this?
- **Regulatory/Compliance Risk**: Does this involve PII/PHI or highly regulated decisions?
- **Change Management**: Will users adopt this system?

## The AI Funnel
Idea Generation → Triage → Proof of Concept (POC) → Pilot → Production Scale.
