# AI Vision

## Vision Statement
*To transition the enterprise from AI-enabled to AI-Native, seamlessly embedding intelligence and autonomous decision-making into every product, process, and customer interaction, driving unprecedented scale, efficiency, and value.*

## Core Principles
1. **Human-AI Collaboration**: AI augments human potential, shifting work from task execution to strategy and governance.
2. **Responsible AI by Design**: All AI systems are transparent, fair, explainable, and secure.
3. **Enterprise Context**: AI is grounded in the enterprise's unique proprietary data and business logic.
4. **Agility through Modularity**: Utilizing a composable AI architecture to adapt rapidly to evolving foundational models.
