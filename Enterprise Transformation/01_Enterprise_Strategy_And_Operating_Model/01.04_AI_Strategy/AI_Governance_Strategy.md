# AI Governance Strategy

## Overview
The framework ensuring AI initiatives are compliant, secure, and aligned with enterprise standards.

## Key Components
- **Model Registry & Inventory**: Complete visibility of all models deployed in the enterprise.
- **AI Gateway/Firewall**: Centralized control point for LLM access, enforcing rate limits, prompt logging, and PII masking.
- **Lifecycle Governance**: Strict stage-gates from Development → Testing → Production.
- **Regulatory Alignment**: Ensuring compliance with emerging regulations (e.g., EU AI Act, NIST AI RMF).
