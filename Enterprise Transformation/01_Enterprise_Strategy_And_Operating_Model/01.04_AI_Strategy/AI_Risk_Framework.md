# AI Risk Framework

## Risk Categories
1. **Data Risks**: Poisoning, privacy breaches, IP leakage.
2. **Model Risks**: Hallucinations, bias, drift, jailbreaks/prompt injection.
3. **Operational Risks**: Unbounded costs (token runaway), system downtime.
4. **Reputational & Legal Risks**: Copyright infringement, ethical violations.

## Mitigation Strategies
- Red teaming and adversarial testing before deployment.
- Input/Output filtering guardrails.
- Financial controls (FinOps) to monitor API consumption.
