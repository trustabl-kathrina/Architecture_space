# Enterprise AI Roadmap

## Phase 1: Establish Control & Enablement (0-6 Months)
- Form the AI Governance Board.
- Deploy secure Enterprise AI Gateway.
- Launch internal, secure ChatGPT-equivalent for all employees.
- Audit and inventory existing Shadow AI.

## Phase 2: Contextualize & Embed (6-18 Months)
- Build Enterprise Knowledge Graph and Vector infrastructure.
- Deploy high-priority RAG applications (e.g., Customer Support Copilot).
- Establish mature LLMOps/MLOps pipelines.

## Phase 3: Autonomous Operations (18-36 Months)
- Deploy multi-agent systems for complex workflow orchestration.
- Transition routine back-office operations to AI agents.
- Integrate AI capabilities into external-facing digital products.
