# Responsible AI Strategy

## Core Tenets
1. **Fairness & Bias**: Ensuring models do not discriminate based on protected characteristics.
2. **Transparency & Explainability**: Users must understand how and why an AI system made a decision.
3. **Privacy & Security**: Safeguarding data used in training and inference.
4. **Accountability**: Clear human ownership for the outcomes of AI systems.

## Implementation
- Mandatory AI Impact Assessments for all new use cases.
- Establishment of an AI Ethics Board.
- Continuous monitoring for model drift and bias in production.
