# Service Model

## Definition
Defines how different teams within the operating model interact with one another. Based heavily on *Team Topologies*.

## Interaction Modes
1. **Collaboration**: Two teams working closely together to discover new solutions (high cognitive load, should be temporary).
2. **X-as-a-Service**: One team consumes a capability provided by another via an API or platform, with minimal direct communication (highly scalable).
3. **Facilitating**: An enabling team assisting a stream-aligned team to adopt a new technology or overcome a hurdle.
