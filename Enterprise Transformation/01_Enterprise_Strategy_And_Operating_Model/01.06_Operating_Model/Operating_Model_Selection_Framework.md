# Operating Model Selection Framework

## How to Choose
Selecting the right operating model depends on the organization's size, maturity, and strategic goals.

## Decision Criteria
1. **Enterprise Size**: Small organizations may thrive on a Centralized model; massive global enterprises require Federated/Mesh models to scale.
2. **Engineering Maturity**: Moving to a Data Mesh requires high maturity in automated deployment (CI/CD) and Platform Engineering. If maturity is low, a Hub and Spoke model is a safer transition step.
3. **Regulatory Environment**: Highly regulated industries may require heavier centralized governance over the federated domains.
4. **Talent Distribution**: If Data/AI talent is scarce, a centralized CoE might be necessary until domain teams can be upskilled.

## Recommendation
Most large enterprises undergoing digital transformation should aim for a **Hybrid (Hub and Spoke) model**, gradually evolving toward a **Data Mesh / Domain-Oriented model** as platform maturity and automation increase.
