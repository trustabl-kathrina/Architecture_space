# Domain Operating Model

## Overview
Structuring the enterprise around bounded business contexts (Domains) rather than technical functions (e.g., DBA team, Frontend team).

## Stream-Aligned Teams
- Cross-functional teams (Product Owner, Engineers, Data Scientists, Designers) aligned to a single continuous stream of work within a domain.
- Empowered to deliver value end-to-end autonomously.

## Examples
- *Customer Onboarding Domain*
- *Checkout & Payment Domain*
- *Fraud Detection Domain*
