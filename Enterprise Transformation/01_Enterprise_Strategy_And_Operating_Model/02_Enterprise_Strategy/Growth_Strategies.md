# Growth Strategies

## Overview
This document outlines how the enterprise intends to grow and how the Data & AI architecture must support these initiatives.

## Key Strategies

### 1. Organic Growth (Core Business)
- **Data/AI Support Requirement**: Hyper-personalization, dynamic pricing models, and churn prediction.

### 2. New Market Penetration
- **Data/AI Support Requirement**: Market analytics, rapid deployment of localized digital platforms.

### 3. Mergers & Acquisitions (M&A)
- **Data/AI Support Requirement**: Standardized integration architectures (APIs/Event Bus) and master data consolidation frameworks to rapidly onboard acquired entities.

### 4. Digital Product Innovation (Data Monetization)
- **Data/AI Support Requirement**: Building external-facing APIs and data sharing platforms to sell insights to B2B partners.
