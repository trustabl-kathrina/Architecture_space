# Competitive Differentiation

## Overview
How the enterprise uses Data and AI to distinctively outperform competitors.

## Differentiation Vectors

### 1. Customer Intimacy
- Using predictive AI to anticipate customer needs before they arise.
- Delivering a unified, frictionless omnichannel experience.

### 2. Operational Agility
- Utilizing real-time data and event-driven architecture to respond to supply chain or market disruptions instantly.
- Automating internal workflows to operate at lower costs than competitors.

### 3. Product Leadership
- Embedding AI directly into the product offerings to create "smart" products.
- Using customer usage data to drive rapid product iteration.
