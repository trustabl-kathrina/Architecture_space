# Enterprise Value Streams

## Definition
Value Streams represent the end-to-end sequence of activities that deliver value to a customer (internal or external).

## Core Value Streams

### 1. Quote-to-Cash
- Flow: Marketing → Sales/Quote → Order Management → Fulfillment → Billing → Collection.
- **Data Need**: Seamless integration across CRM, ERP, and Billing systems.

### 2. Procure-to-Pay
- Flow: Requisition → Purchase Order → Receiving → Invoicing → Payment.
- **Data Need**: Master Data Management (MDM) for supplier and product data.

### 3. Concept-to-Market
- Flow: Ideation → R&D → Product Design → Launch.
- **Data Need**: Market analytics, collaborative platforms, and telemetry data.

### 4. Issue-to-Resolution
- Flow: Customer Query → Triage → Troubleshooting → Resolution → Feedback.
- **Data Need**: Customer 360 view and AI-assisted support agents.
