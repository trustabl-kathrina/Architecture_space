# Enterprise OKRs (Objectives and Key Results)

## Overview
OKRs translate strategic objectives into measurable results to ensure alignment across the enterprise.

## Example Transformation OKRs

### Objective 1: Become a Data-Driven Organization
- **KR 1**: 80% of critical business decisions are supported by automated dashboards.
- **KR 2**: Publish 50 certified Enterprise Data Products to the internal marketplace.
- **KR 3**: Achieve a Data Quality score of >95% for all Tier 1 data assets.

### Objective 2: Accelerate AI Adoption
- **KR 1**: Deploy 3 high-impact Generative AI use cases into production.
- **KR 2**: Train 40% of the workforce on AI Copilot tools.
- **KR 3**: Reduce manual processing time in Finance by 25% via AI automation.

### Objective 3: Modernize the Technology Foundation
- **KR 1**: Migrate 60% of legacy workloads to the cloud.
- **KR 2**: Reduce average infrastructure provisioning time from weeks to hours via self-service.
- **KR 3**: Decrease overall IT operational spend by 15% through FinOps.
