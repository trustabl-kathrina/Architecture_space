# AI Risk Management Operations

## Overview
Integrating AI risk mitigation into daily operations.

## Operational Controls
- **Algorithm Impact Assessments (AIA)**: Mandatory review for high-risk use cases (e.g., hiring algorithms, credit scoring).
- **Continuous Red-Teaming**: Dedicated security teams trying to break or jailbreak internal AI systems.
- **Drift Alerts**: Automated alerts triggering immediate human review if a model's output deviates significantly from baseline norms.
