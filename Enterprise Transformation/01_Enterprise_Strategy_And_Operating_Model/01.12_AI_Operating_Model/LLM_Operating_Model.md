# LLM Operating Model

## Overview
Specific operational guidelines for managing Generative AI and Large Language Models.

## Foundational Model Management
- **Vendor Management**: Continuously evaluating APIs (OpenAI, Anthropic, Google) vs. Open Source models (Llama, Mistral) for cost and performance.
- **Prompt Engineering**: Establishing libraries of tested, secure prompts.
- **RAG Operations**: Managing the ingestion, chunking, and embedding pipelines that feed proprietary context to LLMs.
- **FinOps for LLMs**: Monitoring token usage to prevent cost overruns.
