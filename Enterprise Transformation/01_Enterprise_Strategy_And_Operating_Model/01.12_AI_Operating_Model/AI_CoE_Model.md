# AI Center of Excellence (CoE) Model

## Overview
A centralized group of experts that establishes the foundation for AI across the enterprise.

## Core Responsibilities
- **Platform Ownership**: Building and maintaining the Enterprise AI Platform (e.g., AI Gateway, Vector DBs, MLOps tools).
- **Governance & Ethics**: Defining guardrails, privacy standards, and responsible AI policies.
- **Talent Enablement**: Training and upskilling domain teams to use AI tools securely.
- **Advanced R&D**: Tackling the most complex, high-value, cross-domain AI challenges that require deep technical expertise.

## Evolution
As the enterprise matures, the CoE shifts from *building* all models to *enabling* business domains to build their own (Federation).
