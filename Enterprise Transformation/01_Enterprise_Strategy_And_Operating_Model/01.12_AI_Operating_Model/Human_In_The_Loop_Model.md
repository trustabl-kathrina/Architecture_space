# Human-in-the-Loop (HITL) Model

## Philosophy
AI augments, rather than replaces, human judgment—especially in high-stakes environments.

## Operating Patterns
1. **AI as Advisor (Copilot)**: AI suggests actions; human decides and executes.
2. **Human as Reviewer**: AI drafts the output; human reviews and approves before finalization.
3. **Human as Exception Handler**: AI executes routine tasks autonomously but routes low-confidence predictions or anomalies to a human queue.

## Workforce Impact
Shifting human roles from data entry/processing to exception handling and strategic review.
