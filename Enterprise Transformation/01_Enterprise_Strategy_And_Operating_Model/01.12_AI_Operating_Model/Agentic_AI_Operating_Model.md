# Agentic AI Operating Model

## Overview
The future-state model where autonomous AI agents perform multi-step tasks across enterprise systems.

## Key Operational Challenges
- **Tool Management**: Safely exposing enterprise APIs (e.g., SAP, Salesforce) for agents to use.
- **Supervision**: Establishing "Manager Agents" or Human-in-the-Loop workflows to oversee execution.
- **Blast Radius**: Ensuring agents operate within strictly defined permissions (Zero Trust for Agents) so a hallucination doesn't result in catastrophic actions (e.g., deleting production databases).
