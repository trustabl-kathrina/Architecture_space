# Hub and Spoke Model

## Overview
A specific implementation of a hybrid/federated model. 

## Structure
- **The Hub (Center of Excellence)**: Provides the underlying platform, establishes best practices, sets governance standards, and houses specialized talent (e.g., deep learning researchers, platform engineers).
- **The Spokes (Business Domains)**: Cross-functional teams embedded in business units (e.g., Marketing, Supply Chain) that build and consume data/AI products using the Hub's platform.

## Key Success Factor
The Hub must act as an *enabler*, providing self-service "paved roads", rather than a *gatekeeper* that must manually approve or build every request.
