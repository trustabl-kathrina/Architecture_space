# What is an Operating Model?

## Definition
An Operating Model is the blueprint for how an organization delivers value. It connects the strategic intent (the "why" and "what") with the execution (the "how").

## Core Components
A comprehensive Operating Model encompasses:
1. **People**: Skills, culture, and talent management.
2. **Process**: Value streams, workflows, and methodologies (e.g., Agile, DataOps).
3. **Organization**: Team structures, reporting lines, and topologies.
4. **Governance**: Decision rights, policies, and control mechanisms.
5. **Technology & Data**: The architectural foundation that enables execution.

## Why it Matters in Transformation
Most Data and AI transformations fail not due to technology, but because the existing operating model (often siloed, project-based, and centralized) is incompatible with modern paradigms like Data Mesh, Platform Engineering, and Agile product delivery.
