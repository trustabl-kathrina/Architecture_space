# Ownership Framework

## The Shift
Moving from "IT owns the systems" to "Business domains own the products."

## Data Ownership
- The business unit that generates the data (e.g., Sales generating CRM data) is accountable for its quality, definition, and lifecycle.
- Data cannot be "thrown over the wall" to a central data team.

## Product Ownership
- Every Data Asset, AI Model, and Platform Service must have a named Product Owner accountable for its SLA, roadmap, and value realization.
