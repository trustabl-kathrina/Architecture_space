# Decision Making Framework

## Overview
To prevent bottlenecks, the operating model must clearly define how, when, and by whom decisions are made.

## Principles
- **Subsidiarity**: Decisions should be made at the lowest possible level by the people closest to the work.
- **Speed over Perfection**: Reversible decisions (Two-Way Doors) should be made quickly. Irreversible decisions (One-Way Doors) require rigorous architectural review.

## Frameworks
- **RACI Matrix**: Defines who is Responsible, Accountable, Consulted, and Informed for key processes.
- **Architecture Review Board (ARB)**: For strategic, cross-domain technology and architecture decisions.
