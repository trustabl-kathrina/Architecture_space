# Data Mesh Operating Model

## Overview
A sociotechnical paradigm that fundamentally reorganizes data ownership and architecture around business domains.

## The Four Principles
1. **Domain Ownership**: Business domains own their data end-to-end (from source to analytical product).
2. **Data as a Product**: Domains serve their data as discoverable, trustworthy products to the rest of the enterprise.
3. **Self-Serve Data Infrastructure**: A centralized platform team provides the tools to make domain ownership feasible without requiring deep data engineering skills in every domain.
4. **Federated Computational Governance**: Policies are defined globally but executed locally, ideally automated through the platform (Policy-as-Code).

## Implication
This is a radical organizational shift requiring significant maturity in Platform Engineering and Data Product Management.
