# Governance Principles

## Overview
Rules guiding how policies, standards, and compliance are managed without stifling innovation.

## Core Principles

1. **Federated Execution**: Centralize policy definition but decentralize execution to the domains closest to the work.
2. **Computational Governance**: Enforce governance through automation (Policy-as-Code) rather than manual review boards wherever possible.
3. **Transparency**: Governance decisions, architectural standards, and data policies must be openly accessible to all stakeholders.
4. **Risk-Proportional Control**: Apply heavy governance to high-risk areas (e.g., PII, autonomous agents) and lighter governance to low-risk experimentation.
