# Enterprise Architecture Principles

## Overview
Enterprise Architecture Principles are the enduring rules and guidelines that inform and support the way an organization sets about fulfilling its mission. They provide a framework for making consistent IT and business decisions.

## Core Principles

1. **Business Alignment**: All architectural decisions must directly support and trace back to strategic business objectives.
2. **Buy Before Build**: Prefer commercial off-the-shelf (COTS) or SaaS solutions for non-differentiating capabilities. Reserve custom building for strategic, competitive advantages.
3. **Simplicity over Complexity**: Minimize the number of moving parts. A simpler architecture is easier to secure, scale, and maintain.
4. **Agility and Flexibility**: Systems should be loosely coupled and modular to allow rapid adaptation to changing business needs.
5. **Technology Independence**: Design systems that are not inextricably locked into a specific vendor's proprietary technology where standardized open alternatives exist.
