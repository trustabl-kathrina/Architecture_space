# AI Architecture Principles

## Overview
Guidelines ensuring that the adoption and deployment of Artificial Intelligence is safe, scalable, and value-driven.

## Core Principles

1. **Human-Centric AI**: AI should augment and empower human decision-making, keeping humans in the loop for high-stakes actions.
2. **Responsible and Ethical AI**: Models must be transparent, explainable, and free from unfair bias.
3. **Enterprise Context First**: Generative AI solutions must prioritize grounding (RAG) using proprietary enterprise data over broad, generic knowledge.
4. **Model Agnosticism**: AI platforms should be designed to easily swap foundational models (e.g., via an AI Gateway) as the market evolves.
5. **Continuous Monitoring**: AI models are not static code; they must be continuously monitored for data drift, concept drift, and degradation in production.
