# Data Mesh Principles

## 1. Domain-Oriented Decentralized Data Ownership and Architecture
Data is decentralized and owned by the business domains that are closest to it (either its source or its main consumers). This eliminates the bottleneck of a centralized data team.

## 2. Data as a Product
Analytical data provided by the domains is treated as a product. The domains are responsible for its quality, discoverability, and security. It must be explicitly defined by Data Contracts.

## 3. Self-Serve Data Infrastructure as a Platform
A dedicated data platform team provides domain-agnostic infrastructure, hiding underlying complexity and allowing domains to autonomously build, execute, and maintain their data products.

## 4. Federated Computational Governance
A federated governance team defines global rules (interoperability, security, compliance) which are executed automatically (computationally) by the self-serve platform.
